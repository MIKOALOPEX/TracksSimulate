## Original implementation and artwork

lifecycle, role-based routing, tangent and arc geometry, shared belt surfaces,
configuration synchronization, and material/refund interactions.

artwork is distributed. No Gearwork, Bits 'n' Tracks, Bits 'n' Bobs or Bits 'n'
